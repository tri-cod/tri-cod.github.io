### Week 1 Achievements:

During the second week, I continued expanding my knowledge of Cloud Computing and the AWS ecosystem. I reviewed the fundamental concepts introduced previously and gained a clearer understanding of how different AWS services work together to provide cloud infrastructure.

I spent more time exploring the AWS Management Console and familiarizing myself with its interface. Through this process, I became more comfortable navigating various AWS services and understanding their configuration options. I also reviewed basic AWS CLI commands to improve my ability to interact with cloud resources through the command line.

In addition, I focused on learning more about Amazon EC2 and its essential components, including AMIs, Instance Types, EBS volumes, and Elastic IP addresses. These concepts helped me understand the infrastructure behind virtual machines and how computing resources are provisioned on AWS.

I also continued practicing EC2 instance deployment and management. This included configuring Security Groups, reviewing inbound and outbound rules, connecting to instances through SSH, and learning how to attach EBS volumes. These exercises provided a better understanding of how to configure and manage cloud-based computing resources.

Throughout the week, I also reviewed the relationship between EC2 instances and EBS storage, as well as some basic resource management operations. Although I still needed more practice with certain configurations, the learning activities helped me become more confident when working with AWS services.

By the end of the second week, I had strengthened my foundational knowledge of AWS and improved my familiarity with the Management Console, AWS CLI, and Amazon EC2. This knowledge provided a useful foundation for exploring more advanced AWS services and practical cloud deployment techniques in the upcoming weeks.
