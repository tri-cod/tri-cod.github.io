### Areas for Further Improvement

Despite the progress I made during the internship, I recognize that there are still several skills and areas of knowledge that require further development:

- **Communication skills:** Improve my ability to present ideas, explain technical concepts, and participate more confidently in discussions with teammates.
- **Problem-solving skills:** Practice analyzing challenges from different perspectives and identifying solutions that are both effective and practical.
- **Technical knowledge:** Broaden my understanding of AWS services and other cloud-related technologies beyond the topics covered during the internship.
- **Technical writing:** Improve the structure, clarity, and consistency of my technical documentation to make information easier for others to follow.
- **Time management:** Build better planning and prioritization habits to manage multiple responsibilities and complete tasks more efficiently.

### Overall Evaluation

Looking back, my internship at **First Cloud AI Journey** was an important milestone in my academic and professional development. It allowed me to gain hands-on experience, reinforce the technical knowledge acquired at university, and better understand the expectations of a real working environment.

Beyond technical improvements, the experience also helped me become more independent, responsible, and confident when approaching unfamiliar tasks. The knowledge and skills I gained have provided a solid foundation for my next learning steps and motivated me to continue pursuing a career in **Cloud Computing**.
