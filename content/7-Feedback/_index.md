> In this section, I would like to share my personal reflections and feedback about my experience in the First Cloud AI Journey program. I hope these comments can provide useful insights for the FCAJ team to further improve the program in the future.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

During my time in the First Cloud AI Journey program, I had the opportunity to study and work in an environment that was both professional and welcoming. The members were approachable and always willing to offer assistance whenever I encountered difficulties, including situations that were not directly related to my assigned responsibilities. This supportive atmosphere made me feel comfortable sharing my thoughts, asking questions, and actively exploring new knowledge. I consider this one of the most valuable aspects of the program.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**

Throughout the program, the mentors regularly monitored my progress and provided guidance whenever necessary. Rather than giving direct solutions, they encouraged me to investigate problems independently and understand the reasons behind them. This approach helped me improve my analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities. In addition, the admin team provided timely assistance by sharing study resources, responding to questions, and keeping participants informed about important updates and announcements.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Major**

The assignments and practical activities were closely connected to my academic background in Information Technology and my career interests in Cloud Computing. Through the program, I gained hands-on experience with AWS services, GitHub, Docker, and application deployment processes. These activities helped me connect theoretical concepts learned at university with practical applications and gain a better understanding of how technology is used in real working environments.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

I found the program particularly beneficial for developing both technical competencies and essential soft skills. In addition to expanding my knowledge of AWS and cloud-related technologies, I improved my self-learning ability, time management, teamwork, technical writing, and presentation skills. These experiences have helped me become more confident and better prepared for future professional challenges.

**5. Culture and Teamwork**

The collaborative atmosphere was another highlight of my experience. Mentors, administrators, and interns were all open to exchanging ideas and helping one another. The friendly communication environment made it easy to ask questions and discuss technical issues without feeling hesitant. Furthermore, workshops and knowledge-sharing activities allowed me to gain additional insights from people with practical industry experience.

**6. Policies and Support for Interns**

The program provided a well-structured learning experience through organized materials, workshops, a clear learning roadmap, and various practical assignments. I particularly appreciated the flexibility of the schedule, which allowed me to manage my academic responsibilities alongside the internship. Overall, the program offered a supportive and accessible learning environment for students who want to explore Cloud Computing.

### Additional Feedback

What I valued most about the program was its combination of structured learning and practical experience. Instead of focusing solely on theoretical concepts, I was able to complete hands-on labs, develop projects, and practice deploying applications using AWS services. The mentors provided helpful guidance whenever I needed support while also encouraging me to explore solutions independently. This balance helped me become more proactive and develop stronger problem-solving skills.

### Suggestions & Expectations

One improvement I would suggest is arranging more networking opportunities and interactive activities between mentors and interns. These sessions could encourage participants to exchange technical knowledge, discuss their learning experiences, and establish stronger relationships within the FCAJ community.

### Additional Comments

I would like to sincerely thank the entire First Cloud AI Journey team for providing students with such a valuable learning opportunity. During the program, I gained not only practical knowledge of AWS and Cloud Computing but also improved my professional attitude, independence, and ability to learn new technologies.

I truly appreciate the effort that the team has invested in supporting students throughout their learning journey. I hope FCAJ will continue developing, introduce more practical learning activities, and create even more opportunities for students to explore and pursue careers in Cloud Computing.
